03 / METHOD AND SOURCE

From deliveries to a dashboard

Original Cricsheet JSON → calendar-year filter → regulation-innings aggregation → reconciliation checks → browser filters and charts.

Wides do not count as balls faced; wides and no-balls do not count as legal bowling balls. Byes, leg-byes and penalty extras are excluded from bowler-conceded runs. Run-outs, retired-hurt, retired-out and obstructing-the-field dismissals are not credited to the bowler.

Player registry IDs identify players across matches where available. No-result matches are excluded from the toss/winner denominator. Available source coverage may differ from another IPL dataset.
